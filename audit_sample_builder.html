Rules layered on the risk score

Materiality floor iAlways audit the largest branches by loan book, however good their control score — a big branch should never be skipped on score alone.
Blind random sample iAdds a couple of branches picked at random, to catch risks no parameter is measuring. This is how you get assurance over the whole population, not just where you expect risk.
Force stale branches iPulls in any branch not audited for 2+ cycles. RBI requires that even low-risk units don't stay excluded indefinitely.
